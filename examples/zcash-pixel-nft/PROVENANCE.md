# Shielded Byte #001 — provenance

Generated as an original Zcash-inspired NFT presentation example. This is an unofficial fan concept and does not claim endorsement by the Zcash Foundation or Electric Coin Co.

## Art specification

- Character: hooded digital privacy guardian presenting a gold Z coin
- Medium: crisp 16/32-bit pixel art
- Palette: near-black, charcoal, warm gold, electric cyan
- Environment: encrypted block-city and shielded transaction vault
- Card: portrait 2:3, legendary rarity, edition 001 / 100

## Image prompts

### Subject

Transparent, centered pixel-art privacy guardian with a complete readable silhouette. Black cloak with gold trim, cyan facial glow, and a single floating gold Z coin. No border, text, scene, or watermark. Reserve the top and bottom typography zones.

### Background

Opaque encrypted cyber-vault in pixel art: black block-city, square data chains, abstract proof circuits, distant gold portal, and cyan mist. Quiet center and typography zones. No character, coin, logo, or readable text.

### Line art

Edit the selected subject into sparse black pixel contours on white while preserving its exact pose, placement, silhouette, coin, hands, hood, cloak, and proportions. No shading, hatching, fills, text, or frame.

The artwork was generated with Codex's built-in image-generation tool. Typography is rendered locally from the checked-in card metadata.
