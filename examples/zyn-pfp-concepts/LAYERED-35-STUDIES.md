## Validation demonstrated

- All characters face right at the same 35-degree three-quarter angle with both eyes visible.
- Mole muzzle color contrasts with its Body; Star Mouth is a separate, protruding six-lobed expression with a recessed center rather than a flat symbol.
- Owl Black Hood and Genesis Helm hide both native ear tufts, while Moon Halo keeps them visible.
- Moth Black Hood and Node Headset are fitted around the antenna bases without hiding the antennae.
- Static light reads primarily in the Background and touches the character only as a narrow rim or reflection.
- Base character artwork remains flat and coarse; smooth additional color is reserved for the runtime light renderer.
