# Zyn Owl and Mole Variant Studies

These sheets apply the simplified Moth production grammar to the other two species in the first 4,444-piece Zyn PFP collection.

## Shared generation prompt

- Match the simplified Moth sheet's 3×2 layout, coarse logical pixel size, black presentation field, gold borders, and black/gold/cyan collection palette.
- Design on a 32×48 logical grid and enlarge with nearest-neighbor scaling.
- Use 6–9 flat colors per character, one dominant accessory, and one simple geometric light background.
- Use one identical base silhouette per species; avoid dithering, texture, micro-detail, engraving, ornate jewelry, detailed scenery, and smooth shading.
- Keep character lighting to a 1–2 logical-pixel rim or eye reflection and verify readability at 32–64 px.

## Owl anatomy

The Owl base uses a broad round head, two native triangular feather tufts, two circular eye discs, a tiny diamond beak, and folded wing shoulders. Headgear may fit around the tufts or deliberately occlude them. Black Hood, Miner Helmet and Genesis Helm hide both tufts; Beanie may partially hide them; the remaining fittings leave them visible. Owl traits must not introduce Moth antennae or detailed feather texture.

Variants: Night Watcher, Moon Scribe, Aurora Seer, Prism Oracle, Eclipse Sentinel, and Signal Keeper.

The active Owl production library is now species-scaled to the 1,333-item supply:
90 visual masters versus the Moth's 60. It contains 18 backgrounds, 15 bodies,
11 mouth/beak directions, 15 eye directions, 13 fitted headgear directions and
18 light previews including neutral. The authored boards and atomic 192×192
review slices live in `owl-production-components/`. As with the approved Moth
workflow, final generated Owl NFTs must be authored whole in canvas cells and
sliced; independently generated visual overlays are not recombined.

## Mole anatomy

The Mole base uses a low rounded head, no visible external ears, narrow-set eyes, a flat spade-shaped muzzle, and two broad digging-paw shoulders. Every Body owns a muzzle patch that clearly contrasts with its surrounding body color. The Mouth layer supplies a differently colored expression mark. The eight Mouth choices include a compact six-lobed Star Mouth with a dark recessed center and one-pixel depth; it replaces Buck Teeth to avoid pushing the silhouette toward a rodent. Traits must not create a mouse, rat, bear, dog, or monkey silhouette.

Variants: Vault Digger, Moon Tunneler, Aurora Burrow, Prism Prospector, Zero Miner, and Node Runner.
