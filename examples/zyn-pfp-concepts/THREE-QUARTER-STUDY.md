# Zyn 35° View Study

This comparison tests Mole, Owl and Moth at a shared 35-degree three-quarter angle, all facing right. It is a viewpoint proposal and does not yet replace the front-facing rule in the authoritative collection specification.

Complete trait-stack examples using this viewpoint are documented in `LAYERED-35-STUDIES.md`.

## Controlled setup

- 32×48 logical pixel construction enlarged with nearest-neighbor scaling
- Neutral charcoal body, cyan eyes and no headgear
- Matching dark-teal background with a simple gold-and-cyan light band
- Same camera height, head-and-shoulders scale and 35-degree right-facing angle
- Both eyes remain visible; the far eye is approximately 70% of the near eye width
- Far-side anatomy uses a darker palette value
- A 1–2 logical-pixel cyan rim marks the right-facing plane

## Species signals

- **Mole:** no external ears, projecting contrasting muzzle, tiny eyes and asymmetric digging paws
- **Owl:** overlapping eye discs, projecting diamond beak, visible native tufts and asymmetric wing shoulders
- **Moth:** clubbed antennae, tiny mouthparts, no beak and overlapping folded wing shoulders

## Production consequence

If selected, all three species should face right throughout Collection 01. Mouth, eye and headgear traits remain single metadata properties, but their artwork may contain separate near-side and far-side pixel groups. Mirrored left-facing variants should not be generated.
