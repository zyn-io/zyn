# Owl component generation notes

Mode: built-in image generation using the approved layered Owl reference.

## Locked anatomy and style

- Right-facing three-quarter view at approximately 35 degrees
- Broad rounded head with two native triangular feather tufts
- Two circular eye discs with a large near eye and smaller far eye
- Tiny diamond beak and folded wing shoulders
- Premium chunky pixel clusters with hard edges
- Black, graphite, cyan, teal, cream, violet and Zcash-gold collection palette
- Complete PFPs authored per canvas cell; no independently generated overlay assembly

## Prompt set

1. Fifteen body and material directions in a strict 5×3 canvas.
2. Fifteen eye directions on a controlled charcoal Owl in a strict 5×3 canvas.
3. Eleven mouth/beak directions plus one calibration cell in a 4×3 canvas.
4. Thirteen fitted headgear directions plus two calibration cells in a 5×3 canvas.
5. Eighteen portrait-safe backgrounds in a strict 6×3 canvas.
6. Neutral plus seventeen strong static-light treatments in a strict 6×3 canvas.
7. A targeted 3×2 native-square Light reserve canvas: Aurora, Eclipse and
   Constellation Sweep in the top row, with matched calibration duplicates below.

The eye canvas received one targeted correction to restore the two native feather
tufts while preserving its fifteen eye treatments and grid.

Review previews retain each authored source grid rather than reflowing every
category into a generic five-column sheet. For the final 1,333-item production
run, requested grid dimensions are never trusted without visual and gutter-based
verification; malformed edge cells must be replaced from reserve canvases.

The Light atlas required measured gutter slicing because its six generated source
columns are uneven. This removes the vertical neighbor-background seams that were
visible in review columns 2 and 5 while leaving the other 72 component masters
unchanged.

The original Light canvas also compressed its sixth column to 238px versus roughly
296px for the other columns. Those three cells are rejected rather than stretched.
Aurora, Eclipse and Constellation Sweep are sliced from exact 512×512 reserve cells;
the other 87 Owl masters remain sourced and rendered exactly as before.
