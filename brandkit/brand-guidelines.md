# ZYN Brand Kit

## Brand idea

ZYN pairs a sculpted, leaf-cut `Z` with the permanence of jade metal. The identity should feel precise, private, durable, and quietly progressive.

## Logo system

- **Primary mark:** the three-part, forward-leaning `Z` with leaf-like terminals.
- **Primary lockup:** mark followed by `ZYN` in uppercase.
- **Avatar:** centered mark on an obsidian rounded square.
- **Clear space:** keep at least the width of the mark's narrowest leaf around every side.
- **Small sizes:** use a flat jade or mineral-white mark below 32 px; reserve the metallic rendering for larger display use.
- **Do not:** rotate, outline, recolor with unrelated hues, flatten the proportions, or separate the three pieces.

## Color palette

| Color | Hex | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Jade | `#0B7A61` | Primary brand color and flat logo |
| Mint | `#35E6AE` | Highlights, active states, and restrained calls to action |
| Obsidian | `#06110E` | Primary dark background |
| Mineral | `#F2F1EB` | Light background and reversed typography |
| Steel | `#8A9691` | Secondary text, dividers, and neutral UI |

Use obsidian and mineral as the dominant fields. Jade carries recognition; mint should remain a scarce highlight.

## Typography

- **Display and wordmark direction:** a bold modern grotesk with broad geometric forms, tight spacing, and clean diagonal cuts.
- **Recommended UI family:** Geist Sans or Inter.
- **Data/technical accent:** Geist Mono or IBM Plex Mono.
- Use uppercase with generous tracking for labels. Keep headlines compact and sentence case for readability.

## Material language

- Brushed anodized jade metal with fine directional grain.
- Polished bevels and narrow mint-white rim highlights.
- Matte black soft-touch surfaces and subtle mineral-paper texture.
- Controlled glow only around the hero mark; UI and print applications stay mostly flat.

## Photography and applications

- Dark, close, tactile product photography with directional edge light.
- Minimal environments in stone, black textile, smoked glass, and metal.
- Use the metallic mark for hero moments, packaging, launch imagery, and profile art.
- Use the flat mark for interfaces, favicons, embroidery, and small-format print.

## Brand attributes

Precision. Permanence. Momentum. Privacy.
