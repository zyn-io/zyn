## Long post

Collection Z is changing how it sells.

No lottery. No tickets. No draw.
4,444 items go straight to an open mint, and each one is your membership in ZCaveDAO.

Here is the deal.

**Price**
0.05 ZEC per item.
0.01 ZEC if you are on the whitelist.
At most 20 per wallet, whitelist or not. The ledger counts it, not us.

**Whitelist**
Everyone who applied is in. You get a code, link it once to your Nap wallet on cavebro.win/mint, and that wallet mints at 0.01.
Hold REGI on Arc? Sign with that wallet on cavebro.win/whitelist and get your code straight away.

**Pay in ZEC, or burn**
Pay in ZEC brought into Nap.
Or burn ZCAVE on Solana or REGI on Arc: the burn becomes credit worth what you burned in USD, and the credit pays for your mints.

**Who picks your item? Nobody.**
The order is fixed once, at the opening, from a drand round and a Zcash block that don't exist yet when we announce them. Not us, not you, not a bot. Anyone can recompute the order from the public inputs, and the ledger only mints the next item in line.

**Where the money goes**
Of every ZEC paid: 90% to the collection's floor, 10% to the ZCaveDAO treasury.
The floor is keyless: any holder can redeem an item for its share, and no key can move it.
The treasury pays out only what members vote for.

**What changed, plainly**
The team puts nothing behind burn credit. A mint paid by burning adds an item but no ZEC, so the floor is exactly what the sale brings in ZEC. No promised floor, no promised value.

**What an item gets you**
A seat in ZCaveDAO: proposals and private votes.
Entry to the members' chat, end-to-end encrypted, run on our own open-source den.
A Mole, an Owl or a Moth, and its role if you take it up: Diggers build, Lookouts check, Lanterns bring people in (cavebro.win/dao).

**When**
Opening: [DATE, TIME UTC] at Zcash block [HEIGHT]. The drand round and Zcash block that fix the order will be posted [at least a day ahead].

Read the terms, and check the digest yourself: cavebro.win/terms
Every rule on one page: cavebro.win/rules

Items can lose value. ZEC brought into Zyn sits in a vault held by two of three team-held custodian keys. Nothing here is investment advice.
