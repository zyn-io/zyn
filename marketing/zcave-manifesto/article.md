# The Cave Is Neutral Ground

**Privacy shouldn't depend on which chain you were born on. Why we're building one place where every chain can go private.**

---

## 1. We live in glass houses

Every major chain was built the same way: public by default.

Your balance is public. So is every trade you've made, every wallet you've paid, every payment you've received, and every mistake you'll make next. Not for a year, but forever, readable by anyone with a block explorer and a reason.

Nobody chose this. It was a side effect of building ledgers everyone can check, and the industry has lived with it for fifteen years as if it were a feature. It isn't. Here's what it means in practice:

- Your employer, your landlord and your ex can watch your money.
- Anyone who learns one of your addresses learns your whole history.
- Every order you place is visible before it settles, so bots trade against you.
- Holding something valuable makes you a target the moment it's linked to your name.

We don't accept this anywhere else. Your bank statement isn't pinned to a wall in the town square. Your salary isn't printed on your front door. On-chain, it is.

## 2. Every chain builds its own small room

Each ecosystem now offers its own way out: a mixer here, a shielded pool there, a privacy layer that works for one chain and one token.

But privacy depends on how many people it hides you among. A private room with five people in it isn't very private. When every chain builds its own small room, each one stays thin, each one is easy to watch, and most people never walk in at all because they'd have to leave their home chain to do it.

So privacy stays a niche, and everyone else stays in the glass house.

## 3. A territory that belongs to no chain

The Cave is our answer: one place that isn't any chain's, where people from every chain can go private together.

You come in with what you hold, whether that's ZEC, SOL, ETH or BTC. Inside, it works differently:

- **Holders aren't names.** What you own isn't published next to who you are.
- **Trades settle together.** Orders are filled in batches at one price, so nobody can see yours first and jump ahead of it.
- **Votes are counted, not watched.** The public sees the tally, not which member voted.

The walls are **Zcash**: the chain with the deepest privacy there is, and the one part of this none of us controls. What happens inside is anchored to Zcash so it can be checked by people who don't trust us.

We'll be honest about where it stands today, because overclaiming privacy is the fastest way to lose the people who care about it:

- A small team runs it.
- The operator and the replicas that check the ledger can see more than the public can.
- The vaults are held by team keys, and there's no forced exit yet.

Every step that removes trust from that list ships only when someone other than us can verify it.

## 4. Not hiding: living

The Cave isn't a place to hide from the world. It's where you go to live in it on your own terms: to save, trade, build and vote without performing for an audience.

Privacy isn't a confession that you have something to hide. It's the normal condition of a life. Doors, envelopes, closed curtains and the secret ballot aren't crimes. They're how free people have always lived. Money that publishes itself is the strange exception, not privacy.

## 5. A society, not a product

A territory needs people who keep it. That's **ZCaveDAO**.

Membership is simple: **one Cave item is one membership and one vote.** No tiers and no animal is ranked above another.

Members take on roles by choice, and each role is kept by work anyone can check:

- **Diggers** build privacy tools and apps.
- **Lookouts** run and check the infrastructure: replicas, the public RPC, audits.
- **Lanterns** bring people in, with events, guides and onboarding.

**Cave Projects.** Builder teams doing privacy work, on Zcash or any chain, can join the Cave as projects. A project is public: its name, its team and its milestones. It proves it controls its repo or domain and earns a Cave Project mark on the ledger that can't be transferred. In return it gets code reviews from Lookouts, mentoring from Diggers, demo days from Lanterns, shared legal counsel paid by the treasury (Cave is not a law firm and gives no legal advice), and grants that members vote on and release milestone by milestone. Projects pay a flat service fee and give up nothing else: no equity, no tokens. Projects don't vote: votes stay one per Cave item, and members stay private.

The DAO's treasury has no key. It pays out only what members vote to pay. Votes run on Zcash blocks, which nobody can speed up, and the rules are fixed before the first proposal, so nobody can rewrite them mid-vote.

Moles dig. Owls watch. Moths carry light to people outside.
Different animals, one cave.

## 6. The first door: Collection Z

The first way in is **Collection Z**: 4,444 Moles, Owls and Moths, and with them the first memberships of ZCaveDAO.

It's sold fairly, the boring way:

- **Whitelist first.** Only listed accounts can buy, and everyone on the list draws at the same odds.
- **Tickets, not picks.** A ticket costs 0.01 ZEC and buys a place in the draw, not a chosen creature.
- **Nobody sees early, including us.** Which items go to which tickets is decided by a Zcash block and a public random number that don't exist yet when the sale closes. Anyone can rerun the draw and get the same result.
- **The money has rules too.** 90% of every sale payment goes into the collection's floor, which any holder can redeem against. 10% goes to the DAO's treasury.

The full rules are at **cavebro.win/rules** and the DAO's at **cavebro.win/dao-rules**.
