## Thread: the three roles (one post each, after the article)

**1/3 Moles dig.**
Diggers build privacy tools and apps. The role is kept by work anyone can check: a shipped project or a grant delivered. Paid only by what ZCaveDAO votes to pay.
Take the role → cavebro.win/dao

**2/3 Owls watch.**
Lookouts run and check the infrastructure: replicas that verify the ledger, the public RPC, audits. Kept by a replica that agrees with the public explorer, or a published review.
Take the role → cavebro.win/dao

**3/3 Moths carry light.**
Lanterns bring people into privacy: events, guides, onboarding. Kept by an event or a published guide.
Different animals, one cave.
Take the role → cavebro.win/dao
