For holders of a Collection Z item. Nap shows the door your member pass; the door signs a day token it cannot read, and this device enters with it under a name of its own.

  • Messages, files and voice are end-to-end encrypted (MLS). The den relays what it cannot read; only members' devices hold the keys.

  • Your words are kept on this device. Clearing the browser's data forgets them and your chat name.
  • Selling, moving or redeeming your item ends your entry at the end of the day.
