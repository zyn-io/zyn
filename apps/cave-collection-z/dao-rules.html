ZCaveDAOThe rules

ZCaveDAO is the Cave society: people who build, run and spread privacy, and decide together how Cave's money is used. Its rules run on the ledger; this page says what they are. Vote and propose on the DAO page; your Nap wallet only signs.

01Membership

  • Holding a Collection Z item is a membership. Each item is one vote; holding five is five votes.
  • The animal on an item carries no rank and no role.
  • Selling, moving or redeeming an item ends its membership, which passes to whoever holds the item next.
  • A member pass is a private code made from one of your items: the key to the members' chat (not open yet). It ends when that item moves. Keep it private; whoever has it can use your membership.
  • Later Cave batches are to join the same DAO when they launch.

02Roles

  • Roles are voluntary: take on any, or several. A role adds no votes. It is kept by work anyone can check and paid only by proposals the members pass.
DiggerBuilds privacy tools and apps on Zcash. Kept by a shipped project or grant deliverable. Paid by grants.
LookoutRuns verifying replicas or the public RPC; audits code. Kept by a replica that agrees with the public explorer, or a published review. Paid by running fees and audit bounties.
LanternBrings people into privacy: events, guides, onboarding. Kept by an event with attendance certificates, or a published guide. Paid by event budgets.

03Proposals

  • Any member can propose on the DAO page. The full text (up to 4,000 characters) is published; the ledger keeps its hash.
  • A proposal may ask the treasury to pay an amount of ZEC to an account, or nothing.
  • One of your items backs it first. An item can have one open proposal at a time, and the DAO at most 16 open at once.
  • It needs 10 backing items within 8,064 Zcash blocks (about 7 days) to reach a vote; otherwise it lapses.

04Votes

  • A vote runs 8,064 Zcash blocks (about 7 days). Windows are counted in Zcash blocks, which nobody can hurry.
  • Each item votes once per proposal, yes or no; up to 32 items in one go. An item that changes hands mid-vote cannot vote twice.
  • A proposal passes with more yes than no and at least 10% of the items outstanding when its vote opened taking part. Otherwise it fails.
  • A passed proposal's payment is made by the ledger at the close of its vote.
  • The public sees each proposal, its text, its payment and the tallies — not which items voted. The operator and the replicas that check the ledger can see which item acted.

05Treasury and floor

  • The treasury takes 10% of every Collection Z sale payment (tickets, claims, open mint) and 10% of every resale fee.
  • It is keyless: no key can move it, the team's included. It pays only what a passed proposal pays. Every inflow and payment is on the ledger.
  • It is not the team treasury, which takes the Cave team's own share of resale fees (half of them). Members do not vote on the team treasury, and the team cannot spend the DAO's.
  • The floor is separate and belongs to holders: any item can be redeemed for its share at any time, whatever the DAO decides. See the sale rules.

06What the operator can and cannot do

  • The operator orders transactions, so it can delay or refuse a proposal or a vote.
  • It cannot speed a vote up, change a proposal's rules once it has opened, change the DAO's rules after its first proposal, move the treasury, or mint items outside the sale.
  • On mainnet the rules can never go below 3 backers, a 5% quorum or two days.

07Still to come

  • The charter, agreed by members; the members' chat, opened with member passes; Cave names with roles on them; votes hidden from the operator too (zero-knowledge proofs); a legal wrapper.

Where the rules live. The numbers above are in Collection Z's published sale terms and enforced by the ledger; the ledger's live settings are read on the DAO page. Where this page and the ledger differ, the ledger decides. Membership is not an investment and carries no promise of payment.
