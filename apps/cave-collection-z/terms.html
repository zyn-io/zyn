How the sale runs

  • WhitelistAn approved code, linked once to your Zyn account on /mint, puts that account on the list. The ledger sells tickets only to listed accounts.
  • TicketBought in Nap with ZEC.zy. It exists the moment Nap signs it. The rising price is the ledger's, counted per account.
  • Split90% of what is paid to the collection's pool, 10% to the ZCaveDAO treasury, on the ledger as each ticket is bought. The treasury also takes 10% of every resale fee.
  • DrawRun once over every ticket bought before the announced close, from the announced drand round and the Zcash reveal block. Its result is posted on Zyn as a Merkle root; anyone holding the public inputs can run it again and get the same root.
  • ClaimA winner claims with a proof against that root, at the claim price per item, within 16,128 blocks after the close. Only the wallet that bought a ticket can claim it; nobody can be refused one the draw gave.
  • Open mintAfter the window, what the draw handed nobody and what winners left sell at the open-mint price, in an order fixed by the draw.
  • Burn ticketsBurning the Cave token on Solana also gives tickets in the same draw at the same odds. They pay nothing into the pool; a winning one pays the claim price per item, and only a listed wallet can claim it, within the claim window. Burns after the close, past the cap, or under one ticket get nothing and are not refunded.
  • ZCaveDAOHolders of the collection's items are its members. The treasury pays only on a proposal that passed under the rules above.

What the operator decides. Zyn cannot read Solana, so the operator's burn watcher decides which burns count and at what pool price; the count is checkable against Solana's burns. The operator orders transactions, so it can delay or refuse a ticket, a claim or a vote. It sets the burn cap on the ledger, where anyone can read it; a cap above 20 would break these terms. It cannot change a price or the split once the sale starts, change a proposal's rules, or give itself items. The operator and replicas see which item voted.

Standard-library Python. The domain and the payload are each length-prefixed before hashing. 

from hashlib import sha256

payload = bytes.fromhex(open("terms.hex").read().strip())
domain  = b"cave.sale.terms.v2"
h = sha256()
h.update(len(domain).to_bytes(4, "big"));  h.update(domain)
h.update(len(payload).to_bytes(4, "big")); h.update(payload)
print(h.hexdigest())
# 9267d58e849caf1bff4a622451185c609e476ac0d026e3c95761bee73cae3d3c

What this page does not yet prove

It proves what the terms are and that this digest is taken over exactly these bytes. It does not prove when they were written — nothing inside a document can establish its own age, and this is our own web server.

That takes a stamp on Zcash: the terms digest written into a transaction memo, so the chain dates it rather than us. That has not happened yet, and until it does, treat this page as a publication with our word on the timing.
