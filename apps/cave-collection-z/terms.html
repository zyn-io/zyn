How the sale runs

  • WhitelistLink the code you were sent on /mint, and that Zyn account goes on the list. The ledger charges listed accounts 0.01 ZEC an item.
  • MintBought in Nap with ZEC.zy: the next item in the order, at your price, straight into your wallet. The ledger counts 20 at most per account.
  • Split90% of what is paid to the collection's pool, 10% to the ZCaveDAO treasury, on the ledger as each item is minted. The treasury also takes 10% of every resale fee.
  • OrderFixed once, from the announced drand round and Zcash reveal block, over every item; the chain mints only the next one in it. Anyone can recompute it.
  • Sale creditBurn ZCAVE on Solana or REGI on Arc from /mint, with a code from your Nap, and get credit in this sale worth what you burned at its USD price; it pays for mints before any ZEC.zy. Nothing is behind it: a mint it pays adds no ZEC to the pool. Credit is spent only in this sale, is never paid out, and what is unspent when the sale ends is gone.
  • ZCaveDAOHolders of the collection's items are its members. The treasury pays only on a proposal that passed under the rules above.

What the operator decides. Zyn cannot read Solana or Arc, so the operator's burn watchers decide which burns count and at what price; every burn is on its own chain and every credit on Zyn, so both are checkable. The operator orders transactions, so it can delay or refuse a mint, a burn's credit or a vote. The credit budget is on the ledger, where anyone can read it. It cannot change a price, the cap or the split once the mint starts, pick which item comes next, change a proposal's rules, or give itself items. The operator and replicas see which item voted.

Standard-library Python. The domain and the payload are each length-prefixed before hashing. 

from hashlib import sha256

payload = bytes.fromhex(open("terms.hex").read().strip())
domain  = b"cave.sale.terms.v5"
h = sha256()
h.update(len(domain).to_bytes(4, "big"));  h.update(domain)
h.update(len(payload).to_bytes(4, "big")); h.update(payload)
print(h.hexdigest())
# 25163fef565e4c47cce207bd3b8b0e854acb4df24dab07caa0c142aa38d0dd27

What this page does not yet prove

It proves what the terms are and that this digest is taken over exactly these bytes. It does not prove when they were written — nothing inside a document can establish its own age, and this is our own web server.

That takes a stamp on Zcash: the terms digest written into a transaction memo, so the chain dates it rather than us. That has not happened yet, and until it does, treat this page as a publication with our word on the timing.
