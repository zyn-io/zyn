  • 01The pool is what a redeemed item is paid out of. 90% of every entry funds it.
  • 02Every entry references the terms digest. Anything changed after this is a different sale, not an amended one.
  • 03The close is announced, not fixed here — how long to run is judged by watching demand. Everything the outcome depends on is derived from it by rules already committed.
  • 04The reveal beats the operator: it lands on a drand round and a Zcash block that do not exist when the close is named.

How the sale runs on Zyn

Added 24 Sep 2026. The terms above are unchanged — the same bytes, the same digest, the same collection, odds, prices, close and reveal. What changed is where an entry is paid: on Zyn, from Nap, instead of by a Zcash deposit to the vault.

  • EntryA ticket bought in Nap with ZEC.zy — ZEC you have brought into Nap — at the entry price, 0.01. It exists the moment Nap signs it. The six confirmations apply to bringing ZEC into Nap, not to the ticket.
  • Split90% to the collection's pool, 10% to the treasury, recorded on the ledger as each ticket is bought.
  • DrawRun once over every ticket bought before the announced close, from the announced drand round and the Zcash reveal block. Its result is posted on Zyn as a Merkle root; anyone holding the public inputs can run it again and get the same root.
  • ClaimA winner claims with a proof against that root, at the claim price per item, within 16,128 blocks after the close. Only the wallet that bought a ticket can claim it; nobody can be refused one the draw gave.
  • Open mintAfter the window, what the draw handed nobody and what winners left sell at the open-mint price, in an order fixed by the draw.
  • Burn ticketsA promotional token on Solana may also be burned for tickets — capped, into the same draw at the same odds. They pay nothing into the pool; a winning burn ticket pays the claim price like any other.

Six lines of standard-library Python, no dependencies. The domain string and the payload are each length-prefixed before hashing, so no two different documents can ever hash the same way. 

from hashlib import sha256

payload = bytes.fromhex(open("terms.hex").read().strip())
domain  = b"cave.sale.terms.v1"
h = sha256()
h.update(len(domain).to_bytes(4, "big"));  h.update(domain)
h.update(len(payload).to_bytes(4, "big")); h.update(payload)
print(h.hexdigest())
# f2c3242060baf60e0c193b41dc726b6677d85c158a62ba200c948d48a177a59e

What this page does not yet prove

It proves what the terms are and that this digest is taken over exactly these bytes. It does not prove when they were written — nothing inside a document can establish its own age, and this is our own web server.

That takes a stamp on Zcash: the terms digest written into a transaction memo, so the chain dates it rather than us. That has not happened yet, and until it does a careful reader should treat this page as a publication with our word on the timing. We would rather say so here than have you find out by checking.
