To burn —

A burn cannot be undone. A burn ticket costs nothing into the pool; if it wins, its items are claimed at the claim price like any other. Burns that arrive after the sale closes, or after the burn tickets run out, earn no tickets.

The door is shut. Every ticket is in. The draw waits for a Zcash block and a drand round that did not exist when the sale closed; once both are public it runs once, and anyone can run it again from the same inputs.
