Take ZECback out

The door works both ways. An exit sends ZEC.zy back out as ZEC, to a Zcash address you name in Nap — never on this page, because an address typed into a website is one a website could change.

The door out In your Nap

Exits are signed in Nap, where the key is. Open Nap, switch to Zyn and choose Take ZEC out: name a Zcash address and an amount, and the vault pays it once two custodians sign — usually within minutes. A small bridge fee is added on top, and the Zcash network fee comes out of the amount. The chain records only a commitment to the address.
