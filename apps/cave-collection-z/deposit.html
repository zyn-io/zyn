Bring ZECinto the cave

Everything in here is priced in ZEC — the mint, the floor, every resting offer. This gives you one address of your own to send it to. Not a shared wallet with a note attached, and not a promise to credit you later.

Tunnel mouth In your Nap

Your deposit address lives in Nap, because it is derived from a key only you hold. Open Nap, switch to Zyn and choose Bring ZEC in: it shows the one address that credits you, and the ZEC.zy lands in the same wallet you trade with here.

Open Nap
  • 01Send shielded ZEC to the address Nap shows you.
  • 02It is credited once Zcash confirms it — a few minutes, not instant.
  • 03It arrives as ZEC.zy, the same ZEC, spendable in the cave.

Deposited ZEC is held by the Zyn vault, a threshold account whose shares sit on separate machines. That is custody, and we say so: today you are trusting that vault to honour an exit, the same as any bridge. What you are not trusting is a website — the address is derived from your own key, what the ledger has issued against the vault is public, and an exit is a chain operation rather than a support request.
