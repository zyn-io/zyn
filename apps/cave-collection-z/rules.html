Sale rulesCollection Z

How Cave's first batch, 4,444 items, is sold and traded: from the whitelist to the market. Everything happens on this site; your Nap wallet only signs. The numbers are the ones in the published sale terms (digest 9267d58e…3d3c) and in the ledger's own rules.

01Whitelist

  • Apply on the list page with your X handle and a link to your repost or comment of the pinned post. Asking twice changes nothing.
  • An approved handle gets a code. Link it once to your account on /mint; from then on that account is on the list.
  • The ledger sells tickets only to listed accounts. Everyone on the list is drawn at the same odds.
  • The list keeps your X handle, your link and your note — no email, no wallet or Zcash address. Your account is linked only when you use the code.

02ZEC in and out

  • Tickets, claims and resales are paid with ZEC you have brought in. Bring ZEC in from any Zcash wallet; it counts after 6 confirmations.
  • ZEC can be taken back out to any Zcash address at any time. Two of three custodians sign each payout, and the Zcash network fee comes out of the amount.
  • A 0.5% bridge fee applies each way: on ZEC brought in and on ZEC taken out.

03Tickets

  • Bought on /mint, 1 to 20 at a time. A ticket exists the moment you sign the purchase.
  • Each account's first 10 tickets cost 0.01 ZEC; each one after costs 0.01 more than the last. The ledger counts this per account.
  • A ticket buys a place in the draw, not a chosen item. Nobody — the team included — sees which items a ticket gets before the draw.
  • Of every ticket payment, 90% goes to the collection's floor and 10% to the ZCaveDAO treasury, on the ledger, as it is bought.
  • Tickets are sold in one window. The close is a Zcash block height announced at least 1,152 blocks (about a day) ahead; after it the ledger refuses tickets.

04Paying with ZCAVE or REGI

  • Burning ZCAVE on Solana or REGI on Arc gives credit in the sale worth what you burned at its USD price, at the lower of the last hour's prices.
  • Credit pays tickets, claims and the open mint before any ZEC.zy, under the same whitelist and rising ticket price.
  • The team paid ZEC into the sale for this credit, split like any payment, so items bought with it back the floor like any others.
  • The burn carries a one-time code from your Nap, so the credit is yours without your Solana or Arc wallet naming your account.
  • A burn cannot be undone. A burn past the credit left in the sale gets only what is left; credit is spent only in this sale and is never paid out, and what is unspent when the sale ends is gone.

05The draw

  • Runs once, over every ticket bought before the close, from a Zcash block 100 blocks after the close and a drand round announced with the close (+2,400 rounds). Neither exists when the close is named.
  • Each ticket gets 1 item (80%), 2 (19%) or 3 (1%), and which items, by a shuffle of the frozen 4,444-item manifest (root 0c56593e…8426).
  • The result is posted on the ledger as a Merkle root. Anyone with the public inputs can run the draw again and get the same root.

06Claims

  • A winning ticket claims its items at 0.005 ZEC each, within 16,128 blocks (about 14 days) of the close. 90% of each claim goes to the floor, 10% to the ZCaveDAO treasury.
  • Only the wallet that bought a ticket can claim it, with a proof against the posted root. Nobody can claim for you, and nobody can refuse you an item the draw gave.
  • Each item is minted straight to your account; it shows on your shelf.

07Open mint

  • After the claim window, what the draw handed nobody and what winners left sell at 0.0133 ZEC each, in an order fixed by the draw — the next ones in line, whichever they are.
  • Open-mint payments split like every sale payment: 90% to the floor, 10% to the ZCaveDAO treasury.

08The market

  • List an item on the market at a price in ZEC. A listing says what is for sale and what it costs; it never names the seller. Cancel any time; the item stays yours.
  • The fee is 1% from each side: the buyer pays the price plus 1%, the seller receives the price less 1%.
  • The fees are split on the ledger: 40% to the collection's floor, 10% to the ZCaveDAO treasury (the members', spent only by their votes) and 50% to the team treasury (the Cave team's own share).
  • The item arrives in the buyer's account; the seller's proceeds wait as a claim only their account can collect. Neither side learns who the other is.

09The floor

  • The floor holds 90% of every sale payment and 40% of every resale fee. It is keyless: no key can move it, and it pays only redemptions.
  • Any holder can redeem an item for its share: the floor divided by the items outstanding. The item is burned, which cannot be undone.

10Membership

  • Each item is one membership and one vote in ZCaveDAO, which decides what its treasury pays. Selling, moving or redeeming the item ends its membership. See the DAO rules.

11What the operator can and cannot do

  • The operator orders transactions, so it can delay or refuse a ticket, a claim, a listing or a vote. It announces the close.
  • It decides which burns count and at what price; every burn is on Solana or Arc and every credit on Zyn, so both can be checked.
  • It cannot change a price or the split once the sale starts, mint items outside the sale, give itself items, or move the floor or the DAO treasury.
  • The operator and the replicas that check the ledger can see which account holds which item; the public cannot.

Risk. Items and their floor can lose value. ZEC brought in sits in a vault held by two of three team-held custodian keys, and there is no forced exit yet. Nothing here is investment advice.

Where the rules live. The sale's numbers are fixed by the terms digest on /terms, with the bytes to recompute it; a change to them is a new digest published beside the old one. The market fee and its split are the ledger's. This page explains them; where it and the terms differ, the terms and the ledger decide.
