Sale rulesCollection Z

How Cave's first batch, 4,444 items, is sold and traded: from the whitelist to the market. Everything happens on this site; your Nap wallet only signs. The numbers are the ones in the published sale terms (digest ec3b0c2f…04e8) and in the ledger's own rules.

01Whitelist

  • Apply on the list page with your X handle and a link to your repost or comment of the pinned post. Every applicant is accepted; asking twice changes nothing.
  • On /mint, connect Nap and sign in with X: that account goes on the list. A code you were sent, or one from signing with a REGI wallet, does the same.
  • Accounts on the list mint at 0.01 ZEC an item. The ledger charges the price; nobody can give it to an account that is not listed.
  • Signing in with X reads only your X account's id and handle, once; nothing is posted and no X login is kept. The list keeps your handle, its id, your link and note, and the Zyn account you linked — no email, no Zcash address.

02ZEC in and out

  • Mints and resales are paid with ZEC you have brought in. Bring ZEC in from any Zcash wallet; it counts after 6 confirmations.
  • ZEC can be taken back out to any Zcash address at any time. Two of three custodians sign each payout, and the Zcash network fee comes out of the amount.
  • A 0.5% bridge fee applies each way: on ZEC brought in and on ZEC taken out.

03Mint

  • On /mint, 1 to 20 at a time: 0.05 ZEC each, or 0.01 on the list. You get the next items in the order, minted straight to your account.
  • No account mints more than 20, listed or not. The ledger counts this per account.
  • Of every mint payment, 90% goes to the collection's floor and 10% to the ZCaveDAO treasury, on the ledger, as it is paid.
  • The opening is a Zcash block height announced at least 1,152 blocks (about a day) ahead. An item is your membership in ZCaveDAO.

04Paying with ZCAVE or REGI

  • Burning ZCAVE on Solana or REGI on Arc gives credit in the sale worth what you burned at its USD price, at the lower of the last hour's prices.
  • Credit pays for mints before any ZEC.zy, at your price.
  • The team paid ZEC into the sale for this credit, split like any payment, so items bought with it back the floor like any others.
  • The burn carries a one-time code from your Nap, so the credit is yours without your Solana or Arc wallet naming your account.
  • A burn cannot be undone. A burn past the credit left in the sale gets only what is left; credit is spent only in this sale and is never paid out, and what is unspent when the sale ends is gone.

05The order

  • Fixed once, from a Zcash block 100 blocks after the announced close and a drand round announced with it (+2,400 rounds). Neither exists when the opening is named.
  • It is a shuffle of the frozen 4,444-item manifest (root 0c56593e…8426), posted on the ledger as a Merkle root. The ledger mints only the next item in it.
  • Nobody — the team included — picks which item comes next. Anyone with the public inputs can recompute the order and get the same root.

06The market

  • List an item on the market at a price in ZEC. A listing says what is for sale and what it costs; it never names the seller. Cancel any time; the item stays yours.
  • The fee is 1% from each side: the buyer pays the price plus 1%, the seller receives the price less 1%.
  • The fees are split on the ledger: 40% to the collection's floor, 10% to the ZCaveDAO treasury (the members', spent only by their votes) and 50% to the team treasury (the Cave team's own share).
  • The item arrives in the buyer's account; the seller's proceeds wait as a claim only their account can collect. Neither side learns who the other is.

07The floor

  • The floor holds 90% of every sale payment and 40% of every resale fee. It is keyless: no key can move it, and it pays only redemptions.
  • Any holder can redeem an item for its share: the floor divided by the items outstanding. The item is burned, which cannot be undone.

08Membership

  • Each item is one membership and one vote in ZCaveDAO, which decides what its treasury pays. Selling, moving or redeeming the item ends its membership. See the DAO rules.

09What the operator can and cannot do

  • The operator orders transactions, so it can delay or refuse a mint, a listing or a vote. It announces the opening.
  • It decides which burns count and at what price; every burn is on Solana or Arc and every credit on Zyn, so both can be checked.
  • It cannot change a price, the cap or the split once the mint starts, pick which item comes next, mint items outside the sale, give itself items, or move the floor or the DAO treasury.
  • The operator and the replicas that check the ledger can see which account holds which item; the public cannot.

Risk. Items and their floor can lose value. ZEC brought in sits in a vault held by two of three team-held custodian keys, and there is no forced exit yet. Nothing here is investment advice.

Where the rules live. The sale's numbers are fixed by the terms digest on /terms, with the bytes to recompute it; a change to them is a new digest published beside the old one. The market fee and its split are the ledger's. This page explains them; where it and the terms differ, the terms and the ledger decide.
