Zcash onboarding
Testnet is a copy of Zcash for practice. Its coins, TAZ, are free from a faucet and worth nothing. Every step works the same way. Switch here and in your wallet.

From zero to your first shielded ZEC transaction.

Six steps: set up a wallet, get ZEC, shield it, receive, send, and unshield. About twenty minutes once you have an exchange account, plus waiting for blocks.

You need a phone or computer and a little money to buy ZEC.On testnet you need nothing: the faucet gives you coins.

1Set up a wallet

This guide uses Nap Wallet in its pictures. It runs in the browser, so there is nothing to install. Any shielded Zcash wallet follows the same steps, for example Zodl, YWallet, Zingo! or Noir. The buttons have slightly different names. A list by platform is at the end of this page.

  1. Open nap.zyn-network.com. Pick the testnet network in the menu at the top right.
  2. Choose Create new.
  3. Write the 24 words on paper, in order. Tick the box once they are saved.
  4. Set a password so Nap keeps the wallet on this device. The 24 words restore it anywhere.

Anyone with your 24 words can spend your ZEC, and nobody can recover them for you. Keep them offline: no screenshots, no cloud notes, no chat.

2Get ZEC

Buy ZEC on an exchange that serves your country, such as Coinbase, Kraken or Gemini. Most exchanges pay out only to a transparent address, so that is where you will receive it first.

  1. In Nap, open Receive and choose Transparent address. It starts with t1.
  2. On the exchange, withdraw ZEC and paste that address. Check the first and last few characters match.
  3. Wait for it to arrive. Zcash makes a block about every 75 seconds; exchanges and wallets wait for a few. If your exchange accepts a unified address (u1…), you can withdraw straight to your shielded address and skip step 3. The exchange's withdrawal page says which address types it accepts.

Testnet coins (TAZ) come from a faucet. They are free and worth nothing.

  1. In Nap, open Receive and choose Transparent address. On testnet it starts with tm.
  2. Paste it into the Valar testnet faucet. It pays 0.125 TAZ once a day.
  3. Wait for it to arrive. A block comes about every 75 seconds.

A transparent address is public, like a Bitcoin address. Anyone can see what it receives, from whom, and its balance. Use it to receive from the exchange, then shield.

3Shield

Shielding moves your ZEC from the transparent address into the shielded pool, where amounts and addresses are encrypted.

  1. Once the ZEC arrives, Nap shows it on its own line, Transparent · public, with a Shield button.
  2. Press Shield. Check the fee (0.00015 ZEC for one incoming payment) and confirm.
  3. Nap builds a zero-knowledge proof. It takes about half a minute; keep the tab open. The proof shows the network the transaction is valid without revealing what is inside it. Your device makes it; nothing secret leaves the device.
  4. After a few blocks, about five minutes, the ZEC shows in your shielded balance.

This is your first shielded transaction. The chain shows the transparent address and the amount going in, but not where it went.

4Receive

Your shielded address starts with u1utest1. This is the one to give people. A unified address bundles receivers for Zcash's pools in one string. Nap's holds only a shielded receiver, so the chain never shows this address. A payment that comes from a transparent address still shows its amount.

  1. Open Receive. The shielded address is shown first, with a QR code.
  2. Copy it or show the QR to whoever pays you.
  3. They can add a memo of up to 512 bytes. Only you and the sender can read it; it shows in your activity.

Sharing a shielded address is safe: the chain never shows it as the receiver of a payment.

5Send a shielded payment

From one shielded address to another, the chain shows only that a transaction happened, when, its size and its fee.

  1. Open Send and paste a u1…utest1… address. No one to pay? Use your own.
  2. Enter an amount and, if you like, a memo.
  3. Review and confirm. Proving takes about half a minute.
  4. Copy the transaction id and look it up below.

6Unshield

Sending to a transparent address, for example an exchange deposit address to sell, takes ZEC out of the shielded pool.

  1. Open Send and paste the t1…tm… address. Check it below first if you are unsure what it is.
  2. Enter the amount. Memos only reach shielded addresses, so Nap turns the memo field off.
  3. Review and confirm.

The amount and the receiving address become public. Your shielded balance and where the ZEC came from stay private. Unshield only what you are moving: unshielding the same amount you shielded, soon after, can link the two.
