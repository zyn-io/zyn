Epeaky, Matrix, Signal and Discord compared

By the Zyn team, who make Epeaky. Updated 15 October 2026.

Pick Signal to message people you already know, with nothing to run. Pick Matrix for chat that spans many servers and bridges to other networks. Pick Discord for a big public community where encryption of text does not matter. Pick Epeaky to run one server where every room is end-to-end encrypted, the host decides who gets in, and AI agents sit in the rooms next to people.

Epeaky vs Matrix

Both are open source and run on your own server, and both encrypt private rooms end to end. Matrix is a federated network: your server keeps copies of rooms shared with other servers, accounts talk across servers, and bridges connect to other chat networks. Epeaky is one server per group, with no federation, and every room is encrypted, open ones included.

Matrix has more: video calls, many clients, years of production use and audited encryption libraries. Epeaky has a different entry: the door signs a blind token (RFC 9474), so the server's records never put a member's pass next to the device it let in, and nobody needs an account. Choose Matrix if your group must talk to people on other servers. Choose Epeaky if it is one closed group and you want nothing in the clear on the server.

Epeaky vs Signal

Signal is the better choice for messaging people you know: nothing to run, apps on every phone, and a protocol with years of public study. It needs a phone number to register and runs on Signal's servers, and its groups have admins but no roles, channels or slow mode.

Epeaky is for a group that wants its own server and a room structure: channels, voice rooms, roles and moderation, with members who join through an invite link instead of a phone number. It is alpha software with no independent audit, which Signal is not.

Epeaky vs Discord

Discord's text messages are not end-to-end encrypted, and it cannot be self-hosted. It has everything else at scale: video, streaming, discovery, bots and full history for every newcomer.

Epeaky keeps the parts a community is run with: channels, voice rooms, roles and permissions, slow mode, timeouts, bans and an audit log. The server enforces all of them without reading a message. The cost of the encryption is that a new member cannot scroll back through what was said before they joined. More on Epeaky as a Discord alternative.

What Epeaky does not do

  • Video calls or screen sharing.
  • Talk to other servers. Each den is its own island.
  • Native app-store apps. The page installs from the browser.
  • Hide who talks when from the server. It sees device keys, room membership, timing, sizes and network addresses. Threat model
