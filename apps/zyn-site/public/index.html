How an epoch works

Batched on Zyn. Timestamped on Zcash.

  1. 01

    Sign

    You sign an intent in your wallet. Value is held as notes that only your key opens, not as balances.

  2. 02

    Batch

    The operator orders intents and applies them together, one epoch at a time. Market orders in the same epoch clear at one price.

  3. 03

    Seal

    Each epoch seals into a state root and a journal. ZynScan replays the journal and checks the root.

  4. 04

    Anchor

    Roots are committed to Zcash in anchor transactions. Zcash timestamps the commitment; it does not validate the state behind it.

The trust model

Read this first.

It decides whether anything else here matters to you. These are present-tense facts, not a roadmap.

  • Zyn is operator-sequenced. One sequencer orders transactions. There are no fraud proofs and no validity proofs.
  • Withdrawals depend on the Zyn-operated custodians. Bridged ZEC is backed by ZEC in a 2-of-3 threshold vault operated by the Zyn team.
  • Zcash does not validate Zyn. It records deposits, withdrawals and anchor commitments. Built for Zcash, not of it: Zyn does not inherit Zcash's security.
  • Hidden from the public, not from us. Who holds and trades what is not published. The operator and authorised verifiers can see it today; we are working toward a version where nobody can.

What you can check yourself

Receipts, not adjectives.

zyn_status → issued

The backing

The ledger publishes what it has issued, per asset. The bridge checks every pass that the vault still holds that much, and anyone can compare the same figure with the vault's balance on Zcash, no key needed.

replayed_root = node_root

The replay

ZynScan rebuilds every sealed epoch from the published journal and says it agrees only when its root matches the node's.

anchor transaction

The timestamp

Sealed roots go to Zcash in ordinary transactions anyone can look up. A root cannot be rewritten later without the anchor showing it.

keccak(address ‖ salt)

The exit address

Leaving writes only a commitment to where the money goes. The address is shown to the bridge that pays it, and to nobody else.
