Reading the ledger…

What ZynScan shows, and what it doesn't. Every epoch and every transaction the sequencer accepted, replayed from its journal through the ledger's own rules — the root on this page is recomputed, not copied. Zyn is private from the public at tier 2: for a holder's transaction you see that it happened, its kind and the public object it touched, never an account, an amount or an owner.
